# Black Diamond Brawl

A downhill snowboarding combat racer. Carve down a mountain against four rivals, dodge the trees, catch big air, and fight your way to the finish.

## ▶ [Play it now](https://sidyellur.github.io/black-diamond-brawl/)

**https://sidyellur.github.io/black-diamond-brawl/**

It runs in any modern browser, on a computer or a phone. Nothing to install and no account needed.

On a phone, turn it to landscape. On an iPhone you can tap Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari to get a full-screen icon.

## What you do

- **Race** four rivals down a different mountain every time, or replay one with its seed.
- **Fight.** Hit rivals to stagger them, counter their attacks, or shove them into rocks and trees.
- **Boost.** Near misses, tricks and hits fill a meter you can burn for extra speed.
- **Grab pickups:** a ski pole, a shield, speed wax and a snowball to throw.
- **Read the terrain:** powder slows you unless you jump it, ice makes you slide, and the tree chute squeezes everyone into three lanes.
- **Mind the trees.** The first one is a heavy crash. The second ends your run.

There are five modes: Drop In (a quick race), Practice, a three-race Cup where rivals hold grudges, a Daily Run everyone shares, and a Locker of unlockable boards and jackets.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carve left / right | `←` `→` or `A` `D` | `‹` `›` |
| Jump | `Space`, `↑` or `W` | JUMP |
| Attack | `F` or `K` | HIT |
| Shove | `G` or `L` | SHOVE |
| Boost | `Shift`, `S` or `↓` | BOOST |
| Pause | `Esc` or `P` | PAUSE |
